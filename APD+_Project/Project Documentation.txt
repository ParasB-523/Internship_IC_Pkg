T-SAT DESIGN DOCUMENTATION

PROCEDURE:

1. Firstly, create/get the Bump Maps (Excel files/csv files) of the Dies and the Substrate with compliance to other dies and required specs. Analyze it.

2. Assign the Co-ordinates to the Ubumps and the Balls with a certain pitch and pad dimension, keeping in mind --
	a. the limits of the dies and substrate, i.e. the Dimensions of the die and the bump to edge distance 
	b. the signal integrity and power integrity aspects
	c. usage of the die, bumps and its nature
				, for efficient design.

3. Create the text files of the Co-ordinates and the related bump information for input of Die and BGA symbol creation in Cadence APD+.
The information needed are : Pin No., Pin Name, Padstack, Co-ordinates, Rotation, Pin Use, & Net Name. 
 
4. Then create the mcm Files of all the Dies and Substrate in APD+ using the txt files separately for visualization according to the specifications listed above.

5. Create a total assembled mcm file assembling all the dies and substrate for final Floorplanning and Routing.

6. Place the Dies and Substrate to create the Die Stackup and assign different colours to nets based on their nature to distinguish nets. 

5. Create/Get the Padstacks, STEP Model and the Drawings of the Discrete Components, i.e. in this case, the Resistors, Capacitors and the External Clock and create its respective Device Files.

6. Instantiate the Discrete Components, assign the respective nets to its pins and then manually place them with specific co-ordinates in the remaining surface of the substrate such that the routing is efficient. While Placement keep in mind the Package outline and the covering type so that enough space is left for it.

7.
