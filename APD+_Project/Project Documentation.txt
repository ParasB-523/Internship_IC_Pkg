T-SAT DESIGN DOCUMENTATION

PROCEDURE:

1. Firstly, create/get the Bump Maps (Excel files/csv files) of the Dies and the Substrate with alliance (and analyze it).

2. Assign the Co-ordinates to the Ubumps and the Balls with a certain pitch and pad dimension. Also, keep in mind the limits of the dies and substrate, i.e. the Dimensions of the die and the bump to edge distance for efficient design.

3. Create the text files of the Co-ordinates and the related bump information for input of Die and BGA symbol creation in Cadence APD+.
The information needed are : Pin No., Pin Name, Padstack, Co-ordinates, Rotation, Pin Use, & Net Name. 
 
4. Then create the mcm Files of all the Dies and Substrate in APD+ using the txt files separately for visualization according to the specifications listed in the above steps.

5. Create/Get the Padstacks, STEP Model and the Drawings of the Discrete Components, i.e. in this case, the Resistors, Capacitors and the External Clock and create its respective Device Files.

6. Instantiate the Discrete Components and then manually place them with specific co-ordinates in the remaining surface of the substrate such that the routing is efficient for the concerned nets/signals. While Placement keep in mind the Package outline and the covering type so that enough space is left for it. ( Die to Substrate edge is preferred.)

7. Modify the Nets of the Discrete Components and the concerned pins of the Dies and the Substrate to keep ready the Nets for Routing.
